I am a final-year undergraduate at the University of Wisconsin–Madison, majoring in Computer Science (Honors) and Mathematics.

I work with Prof. [Frederic Sala](https://pages.cs.wisc.edu/~fredsala/) in the Sprocket Lab on generative models of spatiotemporal physical dynamics. I have also worked with Prof. [Yiwei Lyu](https://yiweilyu-tamu.github.io/homepage/) at Texas A&M University on uncertainty-aware planning and safe manipulation, and with Prof. [Chenyu You](https://chenyuyou.me/) at Stony Brook University on EEG foundation models.

Through a PhD in Computer Science, I hope to develop models that learn transferable representations and use uncertainty to reason more reliably about the world. **I am applying to Ph.D. programs for Fall 2027.**

## Research Interests

My research interests lie at the intersection of **multimodal learning, foundation models, and physical AI**, with a focus on learning transferable representations and enabling reliable decision-making under uncertainty. My recent research spans spatiotemporal generative modeling of physical dynamics, uncertainty-aware planning and safe robotic manipulation, and EEG foundation models for neural representation learning. Looking ahead, I hope to explore physically grounded representations and generative world models that generalize across tasks and environments, while investigating how uncertainty can guide learning, planning, and action in complex real-world settings.
